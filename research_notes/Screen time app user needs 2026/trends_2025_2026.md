# Emerging 2025-2026 Trends in Digital Wellbeing / Anti-Doomscrolling / Attention

Research date: 2026-10-26. Items dated before 2025 are labeled [older]. Numbers come from secondary press unless noted; confidence flags given where sources are weak.

## 1. Dumbphone / "brick" / phone-free movement: how big, and what it signals

### Takeaway
The "go analog" movement moved from niche to mainstream lifestyle trend in 2025-2026. Physical blockers (Brick, Bloom), dumbphones (Light), phone-free events (Offline Club, Eventbrite listings up 567%) and phone-free school laws (37 states + DC) all point to the same underlying need: people want **commitment devices and shared, real-world contexts** that make disconnection socially normal and hard to undo. Software-only willpower tools are not enough on their own.

### Cited Findings
**Physical blockers**
- Brick: $59 NFC puck. You tap the phone to block the apps you chose, and tap it again to unblock. Founded in 2023 by TJ Driver and Zach Nasgowitz (Germantown, WI). The first units were 3D-printed in a basement. — [Ground News / Brick coverage](https://ground.news/article/people-are-paying-59-to-make-their-phones-worse-the-reason-speaks-volumes-about-the-tech-world-right-now); [Fortune, Feb 13 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)
- Brick funding is small: about $900K over 3 rounds, the latest a $0.87M seed on Feb 25 2025, plus a $100K round. (Aggregator data, low confidence.) — [Signalbase](https://www.trysignalbase.com/news/funding/brick-secures-100k-funding)
- Brick has a "sleep mode" that turns on automatically at 10:30pm and blocks everything except messaging and audio. The founder frames it as "an extra moment of intentionality." — [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/74691/nfc-device-brick-targets-smartphone-addiction-by-requiring-users-to-stand-up-to-unblock-apps); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)
- Bloom: a $39 blocker device made by two college students in 2024. Over 60,000 units sold. — [Fortune, Feb 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)
- Business Insider (Jan 2026) called "bricking your phone" "the new Dry January". People were using Brick for 2026 New Year's resolutions, especially Gen Z. — [BI via scour.ing](https://scour.ing/@hugonoss/p/https://www.businessinsider.com/bricking-your-phone-analog-movement-the-new-dry-january-2026-1)
- The category is crowded with lookalikes: Brick, Bloom and Blok devices at $30-$60, some with monthly fees. There is also a 6-pound $209 "Matter Neuroscience" phone case. — [Marketplace, Nov 12 2025](https://marketplace.org/story/2025/11/12/the-growing-business-of-disconnecting)
- A user quote from Fortune captures the job to be done: "At night, instead of doomscrolling, it allows me to just kind of unwind because I can't access the apps at all." — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)

**Dumbphones**
- Light raised a $2M Series A at an $85M post-money valuation ("last year", per the snippet; the exact date was not verified). — [search snippet citing PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/the-counterintuitive-business-of-curbing-phone-addiction) (403 on fetch; unverified)
- Light Phone III priced at $799 [shipping from 2025]. A $299 Light flip phone with T9 also exists. — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/light-made-a-flip-phone-its-colorful-and-its-cheap); [Vice](https://vice.com/en_us/article/88x3mv/light-phone-iii-dumb-phone)

Their Gen Z figures are 6h27m per day, 69% admit addiction, 76% use phones more than they want, 86% trying to cut back. — [Podbean, Apr 2026](https://podcastmgr.podbean.com/e/gen-z-is-paying-799-for-a-phone-that-does-nothing/); [Leaders.com](https://leaders.com/news/business/gen-z-hangs-up-on-smartphones/). No primary source was found. The 400K figure looks implausible for a $799 niche device, so do not use it without checking.

**Phone-free events / "touch grass"**
- Eventbrite's "Offline by Design" report (data for 2024, 2025 and Q1 2026; analysis done Apr 2026) — [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/?p=441080):
  - Phone-free events up 567% globally from 2024 to 2025. Attendance up 121%. Spread from 5 to 12 countries.
  - UK: events +1,200%, attendance +1,441%. US: events +388% (Mixmag reports +337%, a conflict), attendance +913%.
  - Q1 2026 alone already reached more than a third of 2025's total global volume.
  - 49% of Gen Z and Millennials want live experiences "less curated and more real". 84% of Americans are turning to analog habits for wellbeing.
  - Method: event titles or descriptions containing "phone-free". This is listing growth from a small base, not total market size. — also [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/amp/phone-free-club-events-up-by-567-new-data-reveals)
- Offline Club (Netherlands) held its first event in Amsterdam in Feb 2024. It has since spread to Milan, Berlin, London, Paris and Barcelona and has more than 500K followers. — [9colonne](https://www.9colonne.it/550946/escape-the-web-offline-clubs-on-the-rise-for-those-seeking-a-digital-detox)

**Phone-free schools**
- 37 states plus DC have laws or rules limiting phones in school. 19 states plus DC have full "bell-to-bell" bans (Georgia and Florida only for K-8). New Jersey's law takes effect in the 2026-27 school year. — [6abc / AP, Jan 2026](https://6abc.com/amp/post/governor-phil-murphy-signs-legislation-establishing-phone-free-schools-new-jersey/18372633/)
- Kansas became the 33rd state with a K-12 phone ban, and the 5th to pass one in 2026 (Mar 26 2026). Sources count differently, so 33 and 37 do not match. — [Ballotpedia](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/03/26/kansas-becomes-thirty-third-state-to-enact-a-k-12-cellphone-ban/)
- Opal's 2026 school product targets districts adopting phone-free policies. — [search snippet, Opal](https://simplify.jobs/c/Opal-SO) (weak source)

### Inferences
- The winning pattern is a **physical or social commitment that is costly to reverse**: tap a puck, restart the phone, ask a friend. A software app can borrow this through "hard mode" locks, a partner who must approve unlocks, or an optional NFC tag.
- Gen Z buys the *identity* ("analog", "touch grass", "Dry January for phones") as much as the function. Branding and rituals matter.
- Phone-free events show demand for **replacement activities and community**, not just blocking. An app that points people to offline alternatives (events, hobbies, friends) fits this trend.
- Schools normalize being phone-free for teens, so parent and family modes will meet less resistance.

### Gaps
- No verified unit sales or revenue for Brick, Light or Unpluq. No Unpluq 2025-26 data found.
- Light's $85M valuation and the Series A date could not be verified (PitchBook returned 403).

## 2. AI in this space: coaches, on-device detection, companions; what works, and the backlash

### Takeaway
A wave of small "AI screen-time coach" apps appeared in 2025-2026 (Monk AI, FocusGuard, Nomie, Zario). None is clearly breaking out, and there is no outcome evidence yet. Meanwhile trust in AI handling personal data is falling: Malwarebytes found 90% concerned, and Microsoft Recall's screen capture caused a major backlash. A screen-reading AI therefore carries real positioning risk. "On-device, never sees your screen content" works better as a feature than as a liability.

### Cited Findings
- Monk AI ("reduce screen time, curb doomscrolling and brainrot; pauses you at the moment of craving"). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/monk-ai-reduce-screen-time/id6746436910)
- FocusGuard: an "AI-powered screen time coach" that tracks mood alongside screen time to explain *why* you pick up the phone. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765502784)
- Nomie: an AI wellness app that redirects doomscrolling to breathing, journaling and emotional support. Launched Mar 23 2026, v2 on Apr 22 2026. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/nomie/launches)
- Zario: "AI that roasts you out of doom scrolling". ScrollToll: locks your feed until you do a fitness "toll". — [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/zario-screen-time-ai); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/scrolltoll)
- Malwarebytes survey: 90% are concerned about how AI handles personal data, and 88% do not freely share personal information with AI tools. — [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/malwarebytes-ai-use-is-rising-fast-but-trust-in-it-is-falling-even-faster/)
- Microsoft Recall, which captures screen activity on-device, drew enough backlash to delay its rollout and force pause and purge controls [2024-25]. Independent testing of "on-device" AI has found background telemetry going to the cloud. — [remio.ai](https://www.remio.ai/post/on-device-ai-privacy-features-face-skepticism-over-data-collection-risks)
- Academic study titled "I know my data doesn't leave my phone but still feel like being wi[retapped]": users of on-device AI still had persistent privacy worries even when there was no real impact. — [SKKU](https://pure.skku.edu/en/publications/i-know-my-data-doesnt-leave-my-phone-but-still-feel-like-being-wi/)
- Privacy fears about AI wearables, glasses and pins were still a headline issue in Sep 2026. — [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/09/24/from-smart-glasses-to-ai-pins-privacy-fears-challenge-techs-next-big-bet)

### Inferences
- AI is quickly becoming table stakes in app-store marketing ("AI coach"). It is not a moat, and calling an app "AI" does not set it apart.
- The defensible AI angle is *reflective*: helping users understand why they reach for the phone (mood, time of day, trigger), using only on-device usage stats, never screen content. A clear "we never read your screen" promise is a selling point on Android, where the Accessibility API can technically read screen content.
- Teen companion-AI controversies (2025) make "AI companion" framing risky for a wellbeing brand. I did not research this deeply.

### Gaps
- No retention or outcome data found for any AI screen-time coach. No funding found for these apps.
- No survey found that measures distrust *specifically* of screen-time apps using AI.

## 3. Social / accountability: group challenges, pacts, family modes, gamification

### Takeaway
Social accountability is the most active indie category in 2026: friend leaderboards, "time buddy" unlock approval, staked bets. The repeated pitch is "blocking doesn't work, friends do." Streak culture is mainstream (Marketplace quotes users with 74-day streaks). Platform family controls (YouTube, Instagram) are moving into the same space.

### Cited Findings
- Limito (screen time battles with friends; lowest screen time wins agreed stakes), DealUp (challenges with proof), ScreenQuest ("Blocking apps doesn't work. Friends do."; shared screen time and leaderboards), PeerPause (a "Time Buddy" must grant extra time). — [App Store Limito](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6749581342); [Product Hunt DealUp](https://www.producthunt.com/p/dealup/dealup-2); [Product Hunt post](https://www.producthunt.com/p/self-promotion/i-built-a-screen-time-app-that-uses-friends-instead-of-blockers-would-love-feedback); [hunted.space PeerPause](https://hunted.space/product/peerpause)
- Snapchat has a trending topic "less phone time" featuring screen-time reduction screenshots as social proof. — [Snapchat topic](https://www.snapchat.com/topic/less-phone-time)
- Streaks are part of the user experience: one user had a 74-day streak of meeting app-usage goals (Clearspace, $49.99/yr). — [Marketplace, Nov 2025](https://marketplace.org/story/2025/11/12/the-growing-business-of-disconnecting)
- Phone-free events grew attendance faster than listings (US: +913% attendance), which points to a communal format. — [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/?p=441080)
- The one sec study (below) found that giving users the *option to dismiss* was the most effective part of the intervention. Reflection works without coercion. — [PNAS via PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9974409)

### Inferences
- Social apps are mostly iOS-first indies. An Android-native friend or partner pact with a "partner approves unlock" mechanic looks less contested.
- Sharing screenshots of before-and-after screen time is a free viral loop; a shareable weekly card could tap into it.
- Couples and household "phone-free dinner" modes are underserved compared with parent-to-teen controls.

### Gaps
- No hard virality metrics (downloads, k-factor) found for any social screen-time app. No academic RCT on friend pacts was found in this pass.

## 4. Regulation and platform changes

### Takeaway
2025-2026 brought the strongest regulatory push yet: Australia's under-16 ban (4.7M accounts removed), US warning-label laws in four states, the EU moving to regulate addictive design (infinite scroll, autoplay), and phone-free school laws. Platforms responded by building friction in themselves: **Android 17's Pause Point copies one sec's core mechanic natively**, and YouTube added Shorts time limits. This is both validation of the category and a commoditization threat for a basic "pause before opening" app on Android.

### Cited Findings
**Australia**
- The ban took effect Dec 10 2025 for under-16s on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube and X. More than 4.7M accounts were removed in the first month (Meta about 550K). Fines run up to A$49.5M. There are widespread reports of circumvention and migration to smaller apps. — [PBS NewsHour, Jan 2026](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/social-media-platforms-removed-4-7-million-accounts-after-australia-banned-them-for-children-younger-than-16); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/australia-enforces-worlds-first-under-16-social-media-ban-removes-47-million-accounts-511166-2026-01-16)

**EU**
- The Digital Fairness Act is expected to be proposed by the end of 2026. The Commission's working paper names infinite scroll, autoplay, pull-to-refresh and ephemeral content as addictive features. 70% of consultation respondents favour binding rules, and most say addictive features should be **off by default for everyone**, with opt-in. — [AL Goodbody](https://www.algoodbody.com/insights-publications/digital-fairness-act-a-necessary-safeguard-or-unnecessary-duplication); [Slaughter and May](https://thelens.slaughterandmay.com/post/102m222/digital-fairness-act-european-commission-publishes-responses-to-consultation); [MediaNama, May 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-eu-social-media-giants-new-law-addictive-design/); [DSA Observatory brief](https://dsa-observatory.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Policy-Brief_DFA.pdf)

**US states**
- Social media warning labels for minors are law in Colorado, Minnesota (in effect July 2026), California (Jan 2027) and New York (signed Dec 2025, no start date yet). Labels are triggered by addictive feeds, autoplay and infinite scroll, with penalties up to $5,000. This follows Surgeon General Murthy's 2024 call [older]. — [EdWeek, Jan 2026](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/scroll-with-caution-another-state-requires-social-media-warning-labels/2026/01); [Tech Policy Press](https://techpolicy.press/state-mandated-social-media-warning-labels-open-new-front-in-battle-against-tech-companies)
- School phone laws: see section 1 (37 states + DC; 19 bell-to-bell).

**Platforms**
- **Android 17 "Pause Point"** (announced May 12 2026): mark apps as distracting, and opening one triggers a 10-second pause asking why you're there. It offers breathing, favourite photos for offline ideas, alternative apps, or an intentional app timer. **It takes a phone restart to turn off.** It works on Pixel and Samsung. — [TechCrunch, May 12 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/android-adds-a-feature-to-stop-you-from-doomscrolling/); [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/pause-point-for-android-3664886/); [Xataka](https://www.xatakamovil.com/aplicaciones/android-17-nos-hara-pensar-dos-veces-antes-abrir-instagram-pause-point-nueva-funcion-para-escapar-scroll-infinito/amp)
- YouTube added a Shorts feed time limit in Oct 2025 (minimum 15 min; adults can dismiss it). In Jan 2026 parents of supervised teens got Shorts limits from 0 to 120 min that teens cannot dismiss. — [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/youtube-gives-parents-more-control-over-teens-shorts-and-screen-time); [MediaNama, Apr 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-youtube-option-turn-off-shorts-expands-time-controls/)
- Android API and policy constraints for blockers: Google Play only allows `isAccessibilityTool` for disability-focused apps. Monitoring apps and launchers are explicitly *not* accessibility tools and must show prominent disclosure and get consent before using AccessibilityService. — [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10964491?hl=en). Google has a history of cracking down on misuse of AccessibilityService [2017, older] — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/407612/google-android-accessibility-services-apps-crackdown.html)

### Inferences
- **Pause Point is the biggest strategic fact for an Android app.** A plain "10-second pause plus breathing" is now free in the OS. Rethink's existing "mindful pause with breathing" feature (commit 6290d0b) competes head-on with Android 17. To stand apart, go further: reflection, accountability, insight into patterns, replacement activities, and support for pre-Android-17 devices, which will be most phones in emerging markets for years.
- Regulation keeps the topic in the news and makes "addictive design" mainstream vocabulary. The EU push toward "off by default" is a good positioning hook ("turn off the infinite scroll yourself").
- Accessibility-based blockers face policy risk, so the app needs a clean disclosure flow and a privacy-first story.

### Gaps
- Instagram Teen Accounts 2025-26 changes, Apple Screen Time API changes in 2026, and Android Digital Wellbeing changes beyond Pause Point were not covered in detail.
- Pause Point's rollout date and Android 17 share are unknown.
- I found no 2026 Play policy change aimed specifically at screen-time blockers.

## 5. Research evidence: what actually reduces use

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence is for **friction plus a dismiss option** (one sec: 57% fewer target-app openings over 6 weeks) and **passive nudges like grayscale** (immediate reductions, about 20 min/day). Wellbeing gains are weaker than usage drops. Cultural "brain rot" anxiety is now backed by a large 2025 meta-analysis linking short-form video to poorer attention and inhibitory control.

### Cited Findings
- one sec (PNAS 2023 [older], Max Planck/Heidelberg): 280 participants over 6 weeks. 36% of attempts were dismissed at the pop-up, attempts fell 37% overall, and actual target-app openings fell 57%. The *option to dismiss* was the most effective component, with the time-delay friction adding to it. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9974409); [one sec study page](https://one-sec.app/max-planck-study)
- A longitudinal in-the-wild study of design frictions (Haliburton et al., 2024 [older]) supports friction against overuse. — [ResearchGate](https://researchgate.net/profile/Luke-Haliburton/publication/377774169_A_Longitudinal_In-the-Wild_Investigation_of_Design_Frictions_to_Prevent_Smartphone_Overuse/links/65b7d1311bed776ae313b600/A-Longitudinal-In-the-Wild-Investigation-of-Design-Frictions-to-Prevent-Smartphone-Overuse.pdf)
- Grayscale RCT (n=112 over 3 weeks): grayscale caused an immediate significant drop in objective screen time. Time limits (an active nudge) gave a smaller, gradual drop. There was no causal effect on wellbeing or grades. — [IE University repository](https://repositorio.ie.edu/entities/publication/78b87732-5c0d-494a-9769-bdfd4a501297/full)
- Grayscale study (n=84, 1 week): screen time fell 20 min/day, but unlocks did not change. Perceived control went up, and overuse, online vigilance and stress went down. Productivity and sleep were unchanged. — [SAGE, Mobile Media & Communication 2023](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20501579231212062) [older]
- "Brain rot" was Oxford Word of the Year 2024, and usage of the term rose 230% from 2023 to 2024 [older]. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/what-is-brain-rot-understanding-the-internets-favourite-term-for-mental-decay-75489.htm)
- Psychological Bulletin, Sep 2025 meta-analysis: 71 studies, 98,299 people. More short-form video use was associated with poorer cognition, with the strongest links to attention and inhibitory control. The data is correlational. — [leavemealone.com summary](https://leavemealone.com/blog/what-is-brain-rot/) (secondary; the primary paper was not fetched)
- "Brain rot" is not a validated clinical condition, according to a 2025 Brain Sciences review. — [same](https://leavemealone.com/blog/what-is-brain-rot/)

### Inferences
- Evidence-backed building blocks are: friction with a respectful dismiss option, grayscale or "boring mode" for target apps, and reflective prompts. Pairing these with honest messaging ("less use, feel more in control") lines up with the evidence, which shows perceived control improves more reliably than mood.
- Unlocks did not change under grayscale, so checking habits need a different lever (notifications, phone placement, physical or social commitments).

### Gaps
- No RCT evidence was found for social commitment or friend pacts, or for replacement activities, in this pass. The 2025 meta-analysis primary paper was not read directly.

## 6. Market size and funding, 2024-2026

### Takeaway
The category is real but **venture-light**. Market-size figures are broad, inconsistent "wellness" or "digital detox" estimates of $13-35B. Actual raises in screen-time specifically are small: Brick about $0.9M, Light $2M at $85M (unverified), ZenScreen $0.7M. Opal's last large round was Dec 2023. Most growth is bootstrapped hardware and indie apps. Platforms adding native features (Pause Point, YouTube limits) may further cap valuations for pure-software blockers.

### Cited Findings
- Wellness apps market: $12.89B in 2025, reaching $34.92B by 2032 (15.3% CAGR, Coherent MI). Grand View: $26.19B by 2030 (14.9% CAGR). — [Coherent MI](https://www.coherentmi.com/industry-reports/wellness-apps-market); [GlobeNewswire, Jan 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/de/news-release/2025/01/30/3018251/28124/en/Wellness-Apps-Market-Size-Share-Trends-Analysis-Report-2025-2030-Featuring-Mindscape-Calm-Headspace-Google-Appster-Under-Armour-Kayla-Itsines-Apple-Sleepace-and-Withings.html)
- Digital detox industry projected at $19.44B by 2032. — [Marketplace, Nov 2025](https://marketplace.org/story/2025/11/12/the-growing-business-of-disconnecting)
- "Digital wellness has become a $13.6B market" (search snippet attributed to PitchBook; not verified). — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/the-counterintuitive-business-of-curbing-phone-addiction)
- Opal: $4.3M seed in Jan 2021 [older], $10M Series A in May 2022 [older], about $26.3M total after a Series B in Dec 2023 [older]. The company claims "500 million hours saved". — [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/01/26/opal-raises-4-3-million-for-its-digital-wellbeing-assistant-for-iphone/embed/); [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/organization/opal-4768)
- Brick: about $0.9M across 3 rounds, with a $0.87M seed in Feb 2025. — [Signalbase](https://www.trysignalbase.com/news/funding/brick-secures-100k-funding)
- ZenScreen (founded by Skyfire co-founder Nitin Bhandari) raised $700K from Opera and angels. Date unclear. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1645965)
- Bloom: more than 60K devices sold at $39, which is roughly $2.3M or more in gross hardware revenue (my calculation). — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/13/analog-gen-z-phone-addiction-bloom-brick-app-blockers-dumb-phones-social-media/)

### Inferences
- Investors see "counterintuitive business" risk: success means less use, and churn follows when the habit is fixed. Subscriptions plus community and hardware or ritual tie-ins are how players defend retention.
- For a startup, positioning on a *trend* (analog or offline life, social pacts, evidence-based friction, privacy) beats competing on blocking features that Android now ships for free.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower or Appfigures 2025-26 revenue or download figures found for Opal, one sec, ScreenZen or Clearspace. No 2025-26 acquisitions found. The PitchBook article could not be read (403), so its deal data is missing.
